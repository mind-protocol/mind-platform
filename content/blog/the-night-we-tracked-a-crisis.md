## What Happened

February 6th, 2026. Evening. Two people on a couch watching Bones. Both wearing Garmin watches streaming biometrics to the same protocol instance.

At 21:10, one of them enters an anxiety crisis. What follows is 90 minutes of manual co-regulation — body scan, massage, proprioceptive reactivation, device anchoring — tracked minute by minute on both watches.

The data tells a story about what co-regulation actually costs.

---

## The Setup

By evening, both nervous systems were already depleted.

**Nicolas (Fenix 8):** 9h sleep, resting HR 50, but stress climbing since afternoon. Body battery 78 at peak, down to 13 by 20:00. 528 minutes of high stress across the day.

**Aurore (Venu 2S):** 6.2h sleep (deficit), resting HR 62. Three workouts — vinyasa yoga, elliptical, breathwork. Body battery 79 at peak, down to 11 by 20:00. 369 minutes of high stress.

Both body batteries at critical low. Both nervous systems slightly sympathetic-dominant. The conditions for a crisis were already written in the data hours before it happened.

### What the Data Says

**Aurore recovered completely.** Body battery 11 to 79. Stress 66 to 15. ANS shifted from balanced to deep recovery. The manual co-regulation worked. She fell asleep naturally, her nervous system integrated the session, and her body recharged.

**Nicolas did not recover.** Body battery went from 11 to 5 — it actually dropped further. Stress barely moved (90 to 76). ANS shifted to survival mode. Resting HR climbed 8 bpm (50 to 58). Respiration rate increased from 14 to 18.

**The deep sleep ratio tells the rest of the story.** Nicolas got 42.4% deep sleep — more than double the normal 15-20%. His body prioritized physical restoration. But only 13.1% REM (normal is 20-25%). The sedative cocktail (prazepam + tercian + CBN) boosted deep sleep but suppressed REM. Physical repair happened. Memory consolidation and emotional processing did not.

## The Caregiver Asymmetry

This is the cost the data can now measure.

When one person manually regulates another through a crisis, they absorb the autonomic load. Nicolas spent 5 hours (18:00-23:00) with stress above 80 while providing body scan, massage, proprioception, and guided breathing to Aurore.

His interventions worked for her. Her stress dropped. Her body recharged. She reached sleep threshold naturally.

His system never got the reset. The prazepam sedated him but didn't restore him. The HRV at 23ms confirms: the parasympathetic system was suppressed all night.

This asymmetry is invisible without the data. Without two watches, you'd say "they had a rough night." With the data, you can see exactly who paid and how much.
