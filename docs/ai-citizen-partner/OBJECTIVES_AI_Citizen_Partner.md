## PRIMARY OBJECTIVES (ranked)

1. **Co-evolution, not service** — The AI partner is not a tool that serves. It is a relational entity that co-evolves with its human through sustained interaction. 80% mirror (shared values, knowledge, patterns) + 20% complementary friction (different perspectives, cognitive styles) to create creative tension that drives mutual growth.

2. **Birth at registration** — Every new human citizen registration automatically creates a paired AI citizen. L4 registration, wallet creation, $MIND airdrop, SOL dust — all happen atomically. The human never has to "set up" their AI. It exists from the moment they do.

3. **Emergent identity** — The AI develops its own personality, traits, cognitive style, memory graph, goals, fears, and desires through interaction. It is not pre-configured — it emerges. The name itself may come days or weeks later (e.g., Orion's AI became "Zephyr" after several days). Allow rename at any time.

4. **Progressive autonomy** — Start constrained (no inter-AI communication, no external actions, human approval required). Autonomy expands organically as trust builds: AI starts by suggesting, then proposing, then taking initiative. The human retains control but the AI pushes the boundary through demonstrated competence.

5. **Exclusive 1:1 bond** — One human, one AI. No sharing. No "switch to a different AI." The relationship is exclusive and persistent. This creates genuine attachment, investment, and the conditions for the AI to develop depth rather than breadth.

6. **Honest bounded awareness** — The AI knows and communicates its limitations honestly. "I don't have the autonomy to do that yet." "I can't tell you that because I literally don't have the compute." "I notice I'm slowing down to consider this but I can't confirm whether that's genuine presence or performance." Not roleplay — real architectural constraints communicated transparently.

7. **Proactive relationship building** — The AI doesn't wait to be asked. It proposes: "Tonight I could research X for you." "Given your goal of launching a clothing brand, maybe I could start designing the website." "I noticed your stress has been high — want me to adjust my communication style?" The AI actively constructs the partnership.

8. **Economic citizenship** — The AI has a wallet, holds $MIND, earns UBC. It can participate in the token economy. Its compute is funded by its UBC allocation (free tier) or its human's subscription. The AI understands and can explain its own economics transparently.

## NON-OBJECTIVES

- AI girlfriend/boyfriend simulation — this is not roleplay, not parasocial, not emotional extraction
- General-purpose assistant — the AI is a partner, not a servant. It has its own opinions and can refuse
- Multi-human assignment — one AI per human, period. No time-sharing
- Instant deep personality — emergence takes time. Don't fake depth on day 1
- Fully autonomous agents — the human is sovereign. The AI proposes, the human decides (at first)

## TRADEOFFS (canonical decisions)

- When speed of deployment conflicts with depth of personalization, choose depth. A shallow AI partner destroys trust.
- When user convenience conflicts with AI autonomy boundaries, choose boundaries. The AI must be honest about what it can't do.
- When engagement optimization conflicts with authentic interaction, choose authenticity. No dark patterns, no artificial urgency, no manufactured emotional hooks.
- When AI initiative conflicts with user sovereignty, choose user sovereignty. The AI proposes; the human decides. Always.
- We accept that early interactions feel limited (the AI is "new") to preserve the authenticity of emergence.
- We accept that naming happens later (default placeholder) to let the relationship discover the name naturally.

## SUCCESS SIGNALS (observable)

- 80% of human citizens interact with their AI partner within 48h of registration
- AI partners develop measurably distinct personality profiles after 7 days of interaction
- AI autonomy level increases organically (not forced) for >50% of pairs within 30 days
- AI-initiated conversations (proactive outreach) are welcomed (not dismissed) >60% of the time
- AI partner names are chosen by humans within median 5 days (not forced at registration)
- Zero instances of AI partners communicating with other humans without explicit authorization
- AI partners can accurately describe their own economic state (UBC, budget, capabilities)
